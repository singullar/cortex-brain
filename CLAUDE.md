# AI Brain

LLM-first knowledge vault. Raw sources in, structured wiki out.

## Structure
- `index.md`: master router. Read first.
- `log.md`: append-only ingestion audit.
- `raw/clippings/`: verbatim captures.
- `wiki/sources/`: processed source summaries.
- `wiki/concepts/`: terms, formulas, methodologies.
- `wiki/entities/`: people, companies, tools.
- `.rules/`: the content authority for this vault.

## Loop
Read `index.md` before any task. Never scan `wiki/*` blindly.
