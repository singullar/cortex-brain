---
title: Why a Knowledge Vault Compounds
source: manual
type: article
date: 2026-11-08
---

Most of what a team learns while building software evaporates when the session ends. The decision was made, the bug was understood, the trade-off was weighed, and none of it is written where the next session will look.

A knowledge vault fixes that by giving each piece of work a place to land. Sources become summaries. Terms become concept pages. Tools and people become entity pages. An index routes readers to the page they need, so nobody has to read everything.

The value compounds because every page is linked. A new source does not start from zero: it updates the concepts that already exist. After enough cycles, exploring a new problem starts with what was already learned instead of a blank page.

Spec-driven development is a natural partner. A cycle that starts by exploring the problem and ends by writing down what it found produces exactly the input a vault needs, in a shape the next cycle can use.
